## Getting Started

A ~~prison sentence~~ journey that begins with punching grass.

> ~~The mod author played GTNH as a kid and was conditioned by its IC2 crop breeding into writing this.~~

1. Craft a Grass Shear and punch grass to get Unknown Seeds.
2. Use a Magnifying Glass on an Unknown Seed to turn it into a Known Seed.
3. Combine a Known Seed with paper to craft a Seed Packet.
4. Craft a Crop Stand from four sticks.
5. Plant the Seed Packet on the Crop Stand.
6. Wait for it to mature. If it won't grow, one of its requirements may not be met; check JEI.
7. For crossbreeding and other mechanics, install GuideME. It's a little complicated, and all the mechanics are documented there.

> ~~I think Unknown Seeds and Known Seeds are a pointless extra step, so I'll remove them later.~~

## Cross-mod Seeds

Only AE2 integration seeds are implemented so far (Certus Quartz, Fluix, Sky Stone, Matter Ball, Singularity).

The rest haven't been written yet (the mods I'd like to support haven't caught up to this version, or I just don't feel like writing them).

If there's a seed you'd like integrated, mention it in an issue and I'll add it right away.

You can add more seeds and crossbreeding recipes yourself. (Documentation is in progress. soon)
